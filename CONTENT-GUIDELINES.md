# Content guidelines — OneRishi

These rules exist because earlier Stitch drafts violated them repeatedly
(fabricated stats, invented Labs projects, an AI-generated portrait used
as if it were a real photo). Treat this as a hard constraint for whoever
implements or extends the site, human or agent.

## Never fabricate

- **No invented statistics.** Not "180+ Field Dispatches," not fake engagement numbers, not made-up percentages. If a real number exists, use it. If it doesn't, describe the work in plain language instead.
- **No invented projects, awards, or achievements.** Every case study, Labs entry, and credibility marker must correspond to something Rushal actually did. When unconfirmed (see Veer Gatha below), show a clearly labeled "pending confirmation" placeholder — never a filled-in guess.
- **No AI-generated faces or people.** Photo slots stay as visibly labeled placeholders until a real photo is supplied.
- **No unrelated stock photography.** No generic "business people," no random objects chosen for vibe rather than relevance.

## Tone

First-person, warm, specific. Not corporate ("we're disrupting X"), not
generic listicle-blog voice. Every blog post needs a real takeaway or
result stated early — write from actual experience and experiments, not
generic advice.

## Positioning

Use "Marketing × Technology × Creativity" as the core framing. Avoid
reducing this to just "Digital Marketer" anywhere on the site.
Photo slots stay as a visibly labeled placeholder rather than substituting a stock image.
